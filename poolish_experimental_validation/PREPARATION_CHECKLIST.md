# Poolish Study Preparation Checklist

Complete and sign one copy for every independent block. Do not edit the preassigned IDs in `POOLISH_81_SAMPLE_MATRIX.csv`.

## Before the block

- [ ] Block ID/date: ________  Operator: ________  Time zone: ________
- [ ] Confirm the block is independent: fresh individual weigh-outs and mixes; not aliquots from a shared mix.
- [ ] Flour brand/type/lot/expiry: __________________________________
- [ ] Fresh yeast brand/type/declaration/lot/expiry/storage: __________________________
- [ ] Record any prior opening and time out of refrigeration for yeast.
- [ ] Confirm scales read to 0.1 g and 0.01 g as needed; check with known weights.
- [ ] Confirm core probe/logger IDs and calibration/check reading in an ice-water check; record result and method.
- [ ] Confirm RT and CT spaces are stable and independently measured; record room and refrigerator readings.
- [ ] Map/record chamber position if gradients are known; prepare a position randomization list.
- [ ] Confirm every container has identical geometry, cover, height scale, and a legible sample ID.
- [ ] Confirm photo setup, ruler, timer, notebook, spreadsheet, sanitation supplies, and power/backup plan.
- [ ] Review the run schedule and ensure one operator can meet all observation and transition readings.
- [ ] If using one probe, stage the run into groups of no more than nine samples and predefine probe reading order.

## Suspension and ingredient setup

- [ ] Prepare fresh 10% w/w suspension: 10.00 g fresh yeast + 90.00 g water.
- [ ] Label suspension with preparation timestamp; homogenize before every draw; discard after session.
- [ ] Use matrix doses: 1.00 g suspension + 99.10 g extra water; 2.00 g + 98.20 g; or 3.00 g + 97.30 g.
- [ ] Confirm the suspension's water plus additional water is exactly 100.00 g for each sample.
- [ ] No salt is used in Poolish. Flour is 100.00 g per sample.
- [ ] Record starting ingredient temperatures and condition CT ingredients/tools consistently.

## Per sample

- [ ] Match sample ID on matrix, vessel, observation sheet, and photo label.
- [ ] Weigh flour, suspension, and additional water independently; record actual values.
- [ ] Mix using the predeclared utensil, action, and duration; record start/end timestamps and deviations.
- [ ] Transfer consistently without tapping/degassing; cover identically.
- [ ] Record mixture core temperature, room temperature, refrigerator temperature, and initial height.
- [ ] Record mixing-end timestamp as elapsed-time zero; start stable phase clock only at target +/-0.5 deg C.
- [ ] Photograph time zero with readable ID and ruler.

## During and after the run

- [ ] Observe at all prescribed timepoints and add readings near a suspected peak.
- [ ] Record core temperature and actual timestamps for every sample at transitions until target +/-0.5 deg C.
- [ ] Keep elapsed, stable-phase, and transition time separate.
- [ ] Record raw observations before choosing maturity classification.
- [ ] Save each photo with sample ID and timestamp; do not reuse filenames.
- [ ] Note room/fridge temperature excursions, opening events, missed reads, spills, and equipment errors.
- [ ] Mark invalid/censored data with a reason; never delete the original row.
- [ ] Confirm the sample summary has no invented or backfilled outcomes.
- [ ] Archive a read-only raw copy before analysis.
