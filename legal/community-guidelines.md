# Pizzabook Community Guidelines

Be respectful. Discuss recipes and techniques without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, or threats.

Share responsibly. Post content you created or have permission to use. Avoid personal data, unsafe instructions, spam, and misleading claims.

Keep the feed useful. Use accurate captions and relevant recipe information. Do not manipulate likes or promote unrelated products and services.

Use the report controls on posts and comments to report violations. Content may be removed and accounts may be restricted or permanently removed for serious or repeated violations.
