# Pizzabook Privacy Policy

## Information we collect

Google sign-in provides a name, email address, profile picture, and provider account identifier. Users may add a display name, biography, equipment details, posts, photos, comments, likes, ratings, and recipe information.

The app may store a session token locally and, when enabled, a push token and installation identifier.

## Use and sharing

We use data to authenticate users, provide community features, send requested notifications, prevent abuse, process reports, and operate the service. Posts, photos, comments, profile name, and profile picture may be visible to other users. Service providers may include Google authentication, cloud object storage, database hosting, and push notifications.

## Account deletion

Users can delete their account from Profile. This removes the profile, posts, comments, and stored photos, subject to limited security or legal retention requirements.

## Contact

Contact the monitored support email listed on the official Google Play page.
